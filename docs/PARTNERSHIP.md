# Partnership strategy — Demo Community–Demo District–Demo Region

NdaY' has selected Demo Community, within Demo District District in Demo Region, as an initial living-lab
setting for its digital public infrastructure initiatives. For Radoko, the strongest next step is a
locally anchored clinical partnership, not additional technology features in isolation.

## The core partnership

- **NdaY'** — applicant and product developer; software, technical integration, documentation.
- **Public health authorities and a participating facility** — clinical-service anchor; named
  physicians, protocol alignment, service permissions, referral pathway.
- **PIVOT** — first-priority health-system partner to approach.
- **Centre ValBio** — complementary community/research partner.
- **A qualified drone operator (e.g. AerialMetric)** — only for a clearly justified operational task.

None of these are committed until they agree. A consortium does not substitute for NdaY's own
eligibility or product readiness.

## The right initial request to PIVOT

> "NdaY' is developing Radoko and wants to establish whether physician-supervised remote
> young-infant assessment addresses an unmet need in your service model. Could we jointly review the
> clinical workflow, existing alternatives, and conditions for a limited feasibility study?"

Ask PIVOT to help answer: what access problem remains; which encounters could receive remote support;
what must still be assessed in person; who obtains reliable household observations; whether this
reduces workload or adds a parallel system; and whether a drone improves the service over simpler
alternatives.

## Modular drone claim

> Radoko's clinical coordination workflow will be evaluated independently of its transport mechanism.
> Drone-enabled support will be retained only where it demonstrates operational value over feasible
> alternatives.

## Bounded initial configuration

One participating facility with named physician coverage; one agreed referral destination and
escalation process; a limited number of communities selected with health partners; a defined
household-assessment method; one clinical research protocol; one accountable data-governance
arrangement.

## What UNICEF needs to see

A locally validated need, named clinical accountability, a feasible examination method, an honest
product demonstration, real participation by mothers, and a costed evaluation plan. A letter of
interest is not research approval, permission to access patient data, or authorisation to fly.
