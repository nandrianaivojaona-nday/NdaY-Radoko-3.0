# Intended use

## Included

- Infants aged 0–59 days in remote households of the Demo Community catchment in Demo District District, Demo Region region, Madagascar.
- Structured caregiver history for core young-infant danger signs.
- Device-assisted temperature and respiratory-rate measurement by a trained assistant.
- Consent-based remote visual observations where image quality is adequate.
- Physician review, signed disposition, caregiver instructions, safety netting, follow-up, referral.

## Excluded

- Autonomous diagnosis, prescribing, or treatment recommendation by AI.
- Replacement of in-person examination when the approved protocol requires it.
- Infants older than 59 days in the first pilot.
- Emergency response (urgent care uses existing referral routes).
- Any claim of measured diagnostic accuracy.

The observation catalogue is a product-team proposal and must be ratified by the clinical partner
against the locally approved IMCI/IMNCI chart before any pilot use.
