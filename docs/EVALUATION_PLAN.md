# Evaluation plan

## Objective

Conduct an appropriately approved, supervised feasibility evaluation alongside standard care. This may
establish whether the system can be tested safely and usefully; it does **not** establish mortality
reduction or population-level diagnostic safety.

## Outcome measures

- **Access** — time from request to physician review.
- **Assessment quality** — completeness and usability of required observations.
- **Clinical evaluation** — agreement with an appropriate reference assessment, including missed danger signs.
- **Referral** — time to action and completion of required referral.
- **Women's experience** — acceptability, privacy, burden, confidence in instructions, participation.
- **Travel** — avoidable journeys and associated time/cost (not all journeys prevented).
- **Reliability** — failed flights, unusable measurements, connectivity loss, interrupted sessions.
- **Economics** — full cost per completed, physician-reviewed encounter vs alternatives.
- **Reusability** — ability of another qualified team to deploy the documented open-source workflow.

Sample size and conclusions must be set with the research partner, not promised in advance.
